One of the MOST important Kubernetes interview topics is ☸️
👉 How do you upgrade a Kubernetes cluster using kubeadm?
Because unlike managed services like EKS...
In a kubeadm cluster, YOU are responsible for everything 🔥
 🧠 Control Plane
 👷 Worker Nodes
 🔄 Upgrades
 🔐 Certificates
 💾 ETCD
That's why every Kubernetes administrator should understand the kubeadm upgrade process.

💡 What is a kubeadm Upgrade?
kubeadm is the official Kubernetes tool used to bootstrap and manage self-hosted clusters.

🧠 Easy Analogy
 ☁️ EKS : 👉 AWS drives the car 🚗
 
 🧱 kubeadm : 👉 You drive the car yourself 🚗
 Including: 🔧 Maintenance, ⛽ Fuel, 🛠 Repairs and 🔄 Upgrades

🎯 Golden Rule:
⚠️ Why Upgrade one node at a time ?
Upgrading a cluster one node at a time is a strategy called a rolling upgrade....
Here is why upgrading a node at a time is the best practice:
✅ Maintain availability
✅ Reduce risk
✅ Enable rollback
✅ Avoid downtime

🚀 Final Takeaway
A successful kubeadm upgrade isn't just about upgrading Kubernetes...
It's about maintaining:
✅ Application Availability
✅ Cluster Stability
✅ Version Compatibility
✅ Zero Downtime
Because in production...
👉 The best upgrade is the one users never notice. 🔥☸️
