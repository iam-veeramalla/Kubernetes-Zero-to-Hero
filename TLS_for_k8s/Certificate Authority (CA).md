👉 What is a Certificate Authority (CA)?
------------------------------------------
Because every TLS certificate in Kubernetes ultimately depends on a CA.
Without a CA...

      ❌ No trust
      ❌ No authentication
      ❌ No secure communication
That's why the CA is the foundation of Kubernetes security 🔥

💡 What is a Certificate Authority (CA)?
-----------------------------------------
A Certificate Authority (CA) is a trusted entity that issues and signs certificates, 
enabling TLS-secured and authenticated communication across Kubernetes components through a zero-trust security model.

🌍 Real Examples of CA
Public CAs:

     🌐 Let's Encrypt
     🌐 DigiCert
Internal CAs:

     🔐 HashiCorp Vault

🚀 Final Takeaway
--------------------
Kubernetes security is built on one simple principle:
 🔒 Encrypt everything
 ✅ Verify identities
 🤝 Trust only authenticated components
And that trust starts with the Certificate Authority (CA) 🔥☸️
